# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We commit to making participation in this project welcoming and respectful for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or affiliation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on methods, evidence, and code.
- Respect privacy, consent, and data-use restrictions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, publication pressure, disclosure of private data, and discriminatory language are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to GuomingLin at [guoming.lin.med@gmail.com](mailto:guoming.lin.med@gmail.com). Reports will be handled promptly and confidentially where possible.
